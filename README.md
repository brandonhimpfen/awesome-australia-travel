> A curated list of resources for traveling in Australia, covering official portals, transport, region guides, national parks, and remote work resources.

Australia is a unique island continent featuring cosmopolitan coastal cities, vast arid outback deserts, ancient Indigenous heritage, and the world's largest coral reef ecosystem. This list is intended for road-trip travelers, wildlife explorers, scuba diving enthusiasts, and coastal adventurers.

## Official & Planning

Essential official portals, visa applications, park authorities, and weather forecasting for Australia.

- [Tourism Australia (Australia.com)](https://www.australia.com/) - Official tourism portal featuring destination maps, trip itineraries, and visa entry overviews.
- [Department of Home Affairs - Visas](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-finder) - Official government portal for tourist visas (ETA, eVisitor, Subclass 600) and entry requirements.
- [Parks Australia](https://parksaustralia.gov.au/) - Federal agency managing Commonwealth national parks, including Kakadu and Uluru-Kata Tjuta.
- [Bureau of Meteorology (BOM)](http://www.bom.gov.au/) - National weather agency providing live forecasts, UV warnings, marine conditions, and fire hazard ratings.

## Transportation

Navigating air routes, interstate rail networks, and regional road transport across the continent.

- [Qantas Airways](https://www.qantas.com/) - National flag carrier with extensive domestic connections across capital cities and regional towns.
- [Virgin Australia](https://www.virginaustralia.com/) - Major domestic airline servicing key urban and regional routes across Australia.
- [Jetstar Airways](https://www.jetstar.com/au/en/home) - Low-cost domestic carrier offering flights to leisure and metro destinations.
- [Regional Express (Rex)](https://www.rex.com.au/) - Regional airline providing transit to smaller rural and outback communities.
- [Journey Beyond Rail](https://www.journeybeyondrail.com.au/) - Long-distance experiential transcontinental passenger trains, including The Ghan and Indian Pacific.

## Destinations & Regions

### New South Wales & Sydney

- [Destination NSW (Visit NSW)](https://www.visitnsw.com/) - Official tourism portal for Sydney, Byron Bay, Blue Mountains, and coastal regions.
- [Transport for NSW (Opal)](https://transportnsw.info/) - Route planner and ticketing guide for Sydney Trains, ferries, light rail, and buses.

### Victoria & Melbourne

- [Visit Victoria](https://www.visitvictoria.com/) - Comprehensive travel guide for Melbourne, the Great Ocean Road, Yarra Valley, and Grampians National Park.
- [Public Transport Victoria (PTV)](https://www.ptv.vic.gov.au/) - Official journey planner and myki public transit card guide for Melbourne and regional Victoria.

### Queensland & Great Barrier Reef

- [Tourism & Events Queensland](https://www.queensland.com/) - Visitor hub for Brisbane, the Gold Coast, Whitsundays, and tropical North Queensland.
- [Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority (GBRMPA)](https://www.gbrmpa.gov.au/) - Official environmental authority offering visitor guidelines and reef protection rules.

### Outback & Northern Territory

- [Tourism NT](https://northernterritory.com/) - Travel guide for Darwin, Alice Springs, Kakadu National Park, and Red Centre outback routes.

### Western Australia & Tasmania

- [Tourism Western Australia](https://www.westernaustralia.com/) - Travel information for Perth, Margaret River, Ningaloo Reef, and the Kimberley region.
- [Discover Tasmania](https://www.discovertasmania.com.au/) - Official tourism authority for Hobart, Cradle Mountain, and island wilderness trails.

## Digital Nomad & Working Holiday

Resources for extended visitors, remote workers, and Working Holiday Visa holders.

- [Working Holiday Maker Program](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-417) - Information on Subclass 417 and 462 visas allowing extended work and travel stays for eligible ages.
- [Digital Nomads Australia](https://digitalnomads.net.au/) - Community platform with guides on internet infrastructure, local tax obligations, and coworking spaces.

## Safety & Cultural Etiquette

Practical advice on bushfire safety, sun protection, coastal water safety, and First Nations cultural respect.

- [Beachsafe (Surf Life Saving Australia)](https://beachsafe.org.au/) - Real-time coastal safety app tracking lifeguarded beaches, rip currents, and marine stingers.
- [EmergencyPLUS](https://www.emergencyplus.com.au/) - Official Australian emergency locator app utilizing GPS/What3Words to direct emergency services in remote regions.
- [Welcome to Country Etiquette](https://www.indigenous.gov.au/) - Principles for respecting Indigenous Australian heritage, sacred sites, and Traditional Owner protocols.
